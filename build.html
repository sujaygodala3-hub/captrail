03 · The setup

What it looks like

Simplified drawing, not to scale.

  1. The microscope’s stand sits in the round dip on the base board, so it’s in the same spot every time.
  2. The finger rest clicks onto the two pegs right next to the stand.
  3. Rest your finger with the nailfold (the skin just behind the base of the nail) under the lens.
  4. Set the height on the stand, then fine focus with the microscope’s focus wheel.

04 · Assembly

Step by step

  1. 01

    Print the cradle and base board

    Print both parts in PLA, lying flat on the bed. Printing them standing up made them curve.

    The pegs are 4.6 mm and press into the cradle’s 6 mm holes; add a dot of glue if they’re loose.

    Print times on our Elegoo Neptune 4 Pro: base board about 2 h 40 min, cradle about 35 min, logo letters about 15 min. The cradle is based on the Attune finger rest by dlx-designlab.

    Download: Base board .stl ↓ Base board .gcode ↓ Finger cradle .stl ↓ Finger cradle .gcode ↓

    The .gcode files are sliced for an Elegoo Neptune 4 Pro with PLA. For other printers, slice the .stl yourself.

  2. 02

    Set up the microscope stand

    Put the microscope in its included stand, lens pointing down. Tighten the stand so the microscope doesn’t drift down.

  3. 03

    Position the cradle

    Click the cradle onto the pegs on the base board and sit the stand in the board’s dip, so the position is the same every time.

    Keep the hand and wrist supported and make sure nothing squeezes the finger.

  4. 04

    Connect and test the microscope

    Plug the USB cable into your computer and open a camera app or our Microscope page. Check that the live image appears.

  5. 05

    Adjust focus and lighting

    Turn the LED down low; bright light causes glare.

    Focus with the focus wheel only, and don’t touch it again after calibrating.

  6. 06

    Test stability

    Lift and reposition your finger a few times. Because the cradle locks onto the pegs, it should land in the same place each time.

  7. 07

    Calibrate measurements

  8. 08

    Capture your first nailfold image

    Put your finger back in the cradle. Use a drop of mineral oil on the nailfold only, no plastic wrap, and avoid irritated or broken skin.

    Keep any glare on the nail, not the skin.

    Use Best of 8 and keep only sharp frames.

    Save images with Enhance off for the dataset.
